STAGE 1

Story Campaign & Narrative Foundations (Est. 30 Hours)

Play through the prologue ('Gone Fishing') and Chapters 1 through 4 at your own pace. Enjoy the dual-protagonist storyline between Jason and Lucia without stress. Most narrative trophies unlock automatically.

CRITICAL ROADMAP CHECKPOINT: Before beginning the final sequence of Chapter 4, create a dedicated manual save slot in the Pause Menu. The mission 'Actions Speak Louder' presents branching faction allegiance choices that permanently lock if not prepared.
STAGE 2

Missable Trophies & Partner Feats (Est. 15 Hours)

Target the 3 missable trophies while partner free-roam mechanics are active. Complete the 8 Stranger & Freaks questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) across Leonida.

  • Sneakthief: Infiltrate a jewelry store during the night without triggering alarms.
  • Retail Therapy: Rob 3 stores back-to-back with partner without losing heat.
  • Hot Together: Clear a 4-star wanted level alongside your partner.
STAGE 3

Collectibles, Activities & Combat Challenges (Est. 25 Hours)

Clear all open-world checklists across the Leonida territory:

  • Perform and land 250 Stunt Jumps ('Cunning Stunts').
  • Earn Gold medals in all 42 Shooting Range challenges ('The Shootist').
  • Check in at 100 WAiNK locations ('You WAiNKer!').
  • Complete 3 full trading card sets ('Don't Touch My Deck').
  • Win at all Top Fun Drone racing spots and beat Cal's fishing record.
STAGE 4

100% Checklist Completion & Platinum Victory (Est. 10 Hours)

Purchase all 6 personal vehicle garages ('Little Boxes'), register 10 rides to the Scooter Bros app, finish laundering $250,000 marked bills ('Dime on the Dollar'), and verify 100% in the Statistics menu for 'A Long Road' and the coveted Platinum 'Vice Got a Price!'.
